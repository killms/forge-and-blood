Welcome, hero

A quick tour of your new life:

  • HERO — your stats, gear, set bonuses, achievements.
  • TALENTS — your specialisation (at lv 15) and 10 tiers of talent picks.
  • BAG — manage your inventory. Tap any equipped slot to swap gear.
  • SHOP — buy sealed crates with the gold you earn.
  • HUNT — pick an expedition and navigate a map of encounters: combat, treasure, choices, boss at the end.
  • ARENA — duel other heroes.

Combat resolves itself — make your decisions outside the fight. HP and MP fully restore between every encounter.
